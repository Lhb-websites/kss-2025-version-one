Frequently asked

Questions.

Do I need to be spending a certain amount on ads? +

No hard minimum, but you'll get the most value if you're actively running campaigns with real budget and real problems to solve. If you're pre-launch or spending under $1k/month, the timing may not be right yet.

What if my question is about Meta or TikTok, not Google? +

Ask it. Primary expertise is paid search but the room covers Meta, Amazon, and TikTok Ads as well. If it's paid media, it's fair game. Other members may also have direct experience with your specific platform.

How fast are async questions answered? +

Operator tier: within 48 hours. Strategist: within 24 hours. Inner Circle: same day. All Monday–Friday. Complex questions get detailed answers. Quick questions get quick answers.

What's the real difference between Strategist and Inner Circle? +

Strategist gets you full group access including hot seats on office hours calls. Inner Circle adds a monthly private 60-min call, direct WhatsApp access, and a quarterly deep-dive audit of your actual account. Inner Circle is capped at 5 members permanently — it never expands.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. Monthly membership, cancel before your next billing date. Founding member pricing is locked as long as you stay subscribed — if you cancel, the rate resets to full price on return.

Why is the group capped at 30 members? +

Because the value of direct access degrades with scale. At 30 members, Q&A response quality, office hours depth, and community signal-to-noise are all at their best. When the cap fills, a waitlist opens — next available spot goes to the waitlist first.
